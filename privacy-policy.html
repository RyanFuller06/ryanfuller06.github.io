Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

clip-bot is a personal tool operated by Ryan Fuller that uploads short, edited highlight videos to the operator's own YouTube channel and TikTok account. This policy explains what data clip-bot accesses, how it is used, and how to revoke access or request deletion.

1. Use of YouTube API Services

clip-bot uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to the operator's YouTube channel. By using clip-bot, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. clip-bot's use of Google and YouTube data is also subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

2. Information we access

When the account owner signs in, clip-bot receives:

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens for the YouTube scopes youtube.upload (upload videos) and youtube.readonly (read the channel's own upload status), and for the TikTok scopes user.info.basic, video.upload and video.publish.
  • The IDs, titles and privacy status of videos that clip-bot itself uploads.

clip-bot does not access viewers' data, comments, subscriber lists, analytics, contacts, email, or any other account data.

3. How we use information

Tokens are used only to upload videos to the signed-in account and to confirm each upload succeeded. Video IDs are recorded so the same video is never uploaded twice. clip-bot does not use this data for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose.

4. How we store and share information

All tokens and upload records are stored only on the operator's own computer, in local files that are not synced or transmitted elsewhere. clip-bot does not sell, rent, or share any YouTube, Google or TikTok user data with third parties. This website and clip-bot do not use cookies or tracking technologies.

5. Revoking access

You can revoke clip-bot's access to your Google account at any time on the Google security settings page (select "clip-bot", then "Remove access"). TikTok access can be removed in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions. After revocation clip-bot can no longer access your account.

6. Data retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while clip-bot is in use. Stored data is deleted within 7 days after access is revoked or upon request. Locally stored API data is refreshed or deleted at least every 30 days, as required by the YouTube API Services policies. To request deletion of any data, email ryanclipfarming@gmail.com; it will be deleted within 7 days.

7. Changes

Updates to this policy will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
