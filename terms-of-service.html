1. About

clip-bot is a personal, non-commercial tool operated by Ryan Fuller that edits livestream highlight clips (vertical format, subtitles, creator credit) and uploads them to the operator's own YouTube channel and TikTok account. It is not offered to the public.

2. YouTube and TikTok terms

clip-bot uses YouTube API Services. Anyone using clip-bot agrees to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and acknowledges the Google Privacy Policy. Uploads to TikTok are subject to the TikTok Terms of Service.

3. Content and credit

Every uploaded video credits the original streamer by name on screen and links to their channel and the original clip in the description. Content is removed promptly at the request of the original creator or rights holder. To request removal, email ryanclipfarming@gmail.com.

4. Acceptable use

clip-bot will not be used to upload content that violates YouTube's or TikTok's Community Guidelines, to impersonate others, to mislead viewers, or to artificially inflate views or engagement.
