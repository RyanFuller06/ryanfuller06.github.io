clip-bot

clip-bot is a personal tool that turns popular livestream moments into short vertical videos with subtitles and on-screen credit to the original streamer, and uploads them to my own YouTube channel and TikTok account.

This app uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Every video credits the original creator and links to their channel. Creators who want a clip removed can email ryanclipfarming@gmail.com and it will be taken down.
